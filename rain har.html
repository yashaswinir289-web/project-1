How It Works

Step 1: Enter your rooftop area.

Step 2: Enter average annual rainfall.

Step 3: Select collection efficiency.

Step 4: Click the Calculate Water button.

Step 5: View the yearly, monthly, and daily estimates.

💡 Water-Saving Tips

• Keep your roof and gutters clean.

• Install suitable filters.

• Use a covered storage tank.

• Inspect pipes regularly.

• Treat and test rainwater appropriately for its intended use.
